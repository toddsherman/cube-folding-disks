# Questions for a mathematical referee

These are the points on which a specialist's judgement would help most. They were drawn up by the AI agents that wrote and checked the manuscript, and until 14 November 2026 they were part of the file `reviews/external-review-packet.md`, which is no longer in this package.

1. **Source model:** Do the retained-corner quotient and physical-disk hypotheses match the intended no-new-cuts source class, including slits ending at boundary fans? Does Lemma B1 (Section 1.6) correctly identify the polyominoes of [ALL25] (squares identified only along edges, vertices only through these identifications), their simply connected polyominoes and their consistent mappings (read as isometric on each square) with physical sources, physical disks and consistent complete-frame maps, and is this the intended reading of consistent mappings?
2. **Universal reduction:** Is the topology in the proof of Lemma R1 complete, namely that P is a PL disk by the classification of compact PL surfaces, that cutting the runs one at a time by (Sch) and the argument of Part 2 of Lemma L2.2 yields the regions as physical disks, including at boundary fans carrying several run endpoints, and that the tangent-join clause of the statement, which (G) uses, holds? This sequential-cut proof replaced, on 9 November 2026, a collar-separation sentence that internal checks had found false at boundary fans with two or more run endpoints. Do reflection plus root normalization remain within the original fixed toggle family?
3. **Minimality:** Are leaf exclusivity, the two-interface coverage-loss identity, the three-occurrence cancellation, and the twelve-arc bound exhaustive? In the proof of Proposition C3, is the transfer of a C2 witness from the normalized representative back to the contracted tree of the minimal member complete?
4. **Finite domain:** Do the face-pair/triple symmetry orbits and all ordered arm lengths cover every remaining path/Y tree? Is the complete-frame action reconstructed correctly by the consumer? Are exact key-set equality and the witness checks (parent array, evaluation on all eight cube vertices, six-face coverage, at most seven active bits) the substantive checks, as Section 3.2 says, and are the nine negative controls (seven semantic corruption controls and two set-comparison illustrations) described accurately?
5. **Layer construction:** Section 4.2 is new as of 7 November 2026, was revised on 9 November 2026, and has been reviewed only by AI agents. Is the straight-cut induction for Lemma L2 complete, in particular the arc-splitting statement (Sch), Lemma L2.2 (each half of a cut is an all-flip physical disk), Lemma L2.3 (any two cells are separated by a cut) and the marker bookkeeping of Lemma L2.4 (tie flags and slides)? Does Remark L2b show that hypothesis (H2) cannot be dropped? Do the cases of Corollary L2a cover every use in L3a and L5–L7, including repeated banks and arbitrarily long interfaces? Do Propositions L5 and L6 deliver six-face maps, as Theorem L7 and Section 5 require, and does (G) give each interface bank as one interval of its region's boundary walk? Are the coupled block-order cases exhaustive?
6. **Realization:** Do the edge ribbons and actual corner-fan links satisfy the simultaneous-cap lemma, with a pointwise epsilon estimate and the correct PL validity definition? In particular, are the explicit epsilon-parametrization in Theorem M3, Lemma M2 for arbitrary disjoint link systems, and Corollary D1's use of [ALL25, Theorem 3.4] complete? Internal checks covered these only by reading or through component bounds, and the exact 3D construction was tested only on small sources. Are the validity definition (ambient isotopy to a trivial folding, that is, to a realization of a flat blueprint) and the blueprint, folded-state and epsilon-correspondence definitions of [ALL25] transcribed faithfully in the Introduction and Section 1.6?
7. **Novelty:** Is an equivalent theorem known under another formulation, or does an existing result imply it? Are the comparison to Question 4.9 and the explicit nonclaims accurate? Is the credit to [ALL25, Section 7] (the tree case, extending [ABDD18, Theorem 4]), [ALL25, Theorem 8.2] and [AAAC21, Section 5] adequate, and is the positioning against [ALL25, Open Problem 9.2 and Conjecture 4.10] accurate? Is Corollary A2, an \(O(N^7)\) decision procedure for simply connected polyominoes, new, and is the credit for the tree case to [ALL25, Section 7] and [LS25] adequate?
8. **Decision procedure:** In the proof of Corollary A2, is the construction of a consistent map from every noncrossing set of cuts complete, including the reuse of the cutting argument of Lemma R1 for sets of cuts that are not the bend arcs of a given map, the ancestor tables, and the composition order of the symmetries? Is the completeness argument through Proposition C3 correct, and are the time bounds and the input convention adequate?
