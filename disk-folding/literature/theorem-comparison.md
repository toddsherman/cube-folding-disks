# Prior-art comparison for the physical-disk theorem

Search date: 5 November 2026; refreshed 7 November 2026 (see “Refresh of 7 November 2026” below and the `refresh_2026_11_07` entry in search-log.json); the decision problem of Corollary A2 was searched on 9 November 2026 (the [ALL25] row below and the `decision_problem_search_2026_11_09` entry in search-log.json), and both results were searched again that night (the `refresh_2026_11_09_night` entry in search-log.json), with nothing found. Candidate: a physical disk (a physical source, in the sense of Section 1.1 of the manuscript, that is homeomorphic to a closed disk) admits a valid layered PL cube folding if and only if it admits a six-face consistent complete-frame map, preserving its source joins and crease set while permitting a different map and layer orders.

Under a literal “homomorphism” reading a 1×6 strip would have a surjective consistent mapping, so the isometric reading is needed. With all joins retained, at 14 cells it covers 258,146 of the 473,908 free non-tree disks that have a surjective map; the other 215,762 (46%) fail its hypothesis. The smallest non-tree disks with a surjective map that fail it have 8 cells (19 free shapes with all joins retained), and an infinite family of rectangles with two tabs has bounding boxes that are arbitrarily large in both directions. For these the candidate reduces the question to the existence of a consistent complete-frame map covering all six faces; it gives no shape classification. *Conjecture 4.10* (CubeFolding in polynomial time): Corollary A2 of the manuscript decides simply connected polyominoes in \(O(N^7)\) time, conditional, like Theorem A, on the computer-checked finite claim C2; the conjecture stays open for polyominoes with holes. The tree case was already polynomial by the classification of Section 7, as [LS25, Section 1] notes. A search on 9 November 2026 found no earlier polynomial-time algorithm for simply connected polyominoes whose cell-adjacency graphs have cycles (`../disk-folding-checks/9-poly-time/NOTE.md`, outside this package). The proposed contribution is the exact map-existence equivalence for all physical disks. Its proof is the chain from an arbitrary surjective disk map to a bounded whole-arc tree, through exhaustive positive certificates, to compatible layers on the unchanged source. The PL validity criterion [ALL25, Theorem 3.4] is not claimed as new; the bank-order lemma (§4.2) is proved directly. No individual instance is claimed as new. All 34,697 small witnesses outside the stated theorems (non-tree disks with a surjective map that fail the hypothesis of [ALL25, Theorem 8.2], with all joins up to 12 cells and with slits up to 10 cells) also fold by elementary moves of the kind [ALL25] uses: 34,648 of them (99.86%) by planar simple folds followed by folding a cube net, and the other 49 by a multi-layer tree wrap whose certification relies on Theorem M3. No example is known that needs the candidate's machinery; the contribution is the universal statement. Wording such as “first classification of all foldable polyominoes” would be misleading.

## Assessment and boundary of this investigation

No inspected primary source states or proves the candidate theorem in the same source and folding model. The result is therefore a plausible new contribution supported by a focused prior-art comparison. The 7 November 2026 refresh found no prior or concurrent resolution of the disk case of Question 4.9 and no known theorem that implies the candidate; the published [ALL25] (16 November 2025) still lists Question 4.9 as open. Two parts were already known: the tree case, essentially from [ALL25, Section 7], and the disks satisfying the hypothesis of [ALL25, Theorem 8.2]. This is not a guarantee of priority: unindexed, unpublished, recent, or differently formulated work could remain. The main residual risks are work under review, the databases that blocked automated access, and the TU Graz thesis repositories.

Before claiming external specialist approval, obtain a written response from a qualified researcher who has examined the statement and its closest predecessors. The current comparison and the manuscript have been prepared for that review; no human reviewer has supplied such a response in this work session.
